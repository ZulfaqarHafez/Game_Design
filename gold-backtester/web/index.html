What can I write?

Sections: params (numbers you tune), vars (named expressions), long/short with when, order (market, stop, limit), price, sl/tp (prices), sl_dist/tp_dist (distances), tp_r (target as a multiple of risk), trail_dist; exit with long_when, short_when, when, max_bars.

Indicators: sma ema rma wma hma rsi atr stdev highest lowest change roc cci stoch bb_upper bb_lower bb_mid kc_upper kc_lower macd macd_signal macd_hist adx plus_di minus_di supertrend supertrend_dir vwap. Signals: crossover crossunder cross barssince valuewhen. Time: in_session("07:00-11:00", "Europe/London"), session_high(...), session_low(...), hour(tz), weekday(tz). Higher timeframe: security("1d", "atr(14)"). Previous bar: close[1]. Position: is_flat, is_long, is_short, bars_in_trade, trades_today.

Trade results in R

R = profit ÷ the money at risk at entry (entry to initial stop). −1R is a full stop-out.

Profit by entry hour and weekday

Each cell sums the P&L of trades opened in that hour. Tap a cell to list those trades.

Runs the readiness checklist: costs ×1.5, pessimistic fills, recent out-of-sample period, Monte Carlo, parameter stability, look-ahead test and the Deflated Sharpe ratio. Takes 5–40 seconds.

Every run you make is listed here (kept in this browser). Trying many variants and keeping the best is how backtests fool people: the checklist's Deflated Sharpe ratio corrects for it.
