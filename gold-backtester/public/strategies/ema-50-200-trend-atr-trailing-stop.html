EMA 50/200 trend, ATR trailing stop

Classic trend following. Go long when the 50 EMA crosses above the 200 EMA, short when it crosses below. A 3x ATR stop trails behind the best price. The opposite cross reverses the position.

Evidence: Trend family has peer-reviewed support (time-series momentum, Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012); these exact parameters for gold are unproven.
