What the research found

  • TradingView licenses its data for display only and bans accounts that scrape it, so this lab copies TradingView's published fill rules and metric formulas and uses Dukascopy's free bid/ask history instead.
  • Trend-following has peer-reviewed support for gold as part of diversified portfolios. Session breakouts and RSI mean reversion on gold are mostly folklore.
  • Costs decide most intraday results. Overnight swap decides many swing results.
  • Count every variant you try. The Deflated Sharpe check uses that count to separate an edge from luck.

Add your own strategy

  1. python -m goldlab new "My idea" --tf 15m creates a strategy card.
  2. Write the rules in plain expressions: crossover(ema(close, 20), ema(close, 50)).
  3. python -m goldlab validate my_idea runs the checklist locally.
  4. Or open the backtester, edit the rules in Settings → Rules, and save them in your browser.
  5. Commit the card to strategies/ and the site lists it here permanently.
